The RULES are:

1. Every book has exactly one topic
    -A book must have a topic.
    -A book cannot have more than one topic.

2. Every book must be stored exactly once on a shelf
    -Each book must have exactly one `storedOn` relationship.
    -A book cannot be assigned to multiple shelves.

3. A shelf contains books of only one topic
    -The topic of every book stored on a shelf must match the shelf's `shelfTopic`.
    -Therefore, a shelf cannot contain books from different topics.

4. Shelf capacity
    -The number of books stored on a shelf must not exceed its capacity.

5. Every shelf has exactly one topic
    -Each shelf must have exactly one shelfTopic relationship.  
    -A shelf cannot have multiple topics.
